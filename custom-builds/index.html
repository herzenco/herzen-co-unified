What dedicated ownership looks like

The engagement is shaped around the project, but the operating standard stays the same.

A clear mandate

Visible priorities

Turn competing requests into a practical plan with milestones, owners, and tradeoffs.

Active coordination

Keep stakeholders and delivery teams connected while risks and decisions are still manageable.

Follow-through
